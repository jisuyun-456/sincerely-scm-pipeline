## 에피소드 스토리 개요

### EP61 "Day's First Project"
**Story:** Day의 Seoul 컨설팅 프로젝트 첫 주. 새벽 5시에 일어나 일하는 Day를 Jason이 목격. 커피 만들어주며 짧은 대화. 저녁엔 Day가 지쳤지만 살아있는 표정으로 돌아옴.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **5am** — Jason wakes to find Day already at her laptop, Seoul call in progress. He makes coffee silently.
2. **Evening** — Day comes back from her "mental Seoul" exhausted but bright.
3. **Before Sleep** — Jason: "That's what you look like when you're running at full speed."

**Key Expression Area:** early mornings / pull through / in the zone / show up / make it work / dedicate

---

### EP62 "The New Hire"
**Story:** FreshLine에 신입 직원 Priya 입사. Jason이 온보딩 담당. 1년 전 자신이 얼마나 압도되어 있었는지 기억하며, 지금은 그 역할을 하는 자신을 발견.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **First Day** — Jason shows Priya around. Recognizes his own early anxiety in her face.
2. **Lunch** — Jason tells her one true thing about the first year (no sugarcoating).
3. **With David (call)** — Jason tells David: "I became the person I needed when I arrived."

**Key Expression Area:** pay it forward / show the ropes / figure it out / remind someone of / been there / mentor

---

### EP63 "Sana at the Park"
**Story:** 주말 공원. 다른 아이들이 Sana에게 영어로 말 걸고, Sana가 한국어와 영어를 섞어서 대답. 엄마들이 Jason에게 "She's bilingual?" Jason이 대답하며 생각하는 것들.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Moment** — Another child asks Sana something. Sana mixes Korean and English in reply.
2. **The Moms** — A parent notices and asks Jason about it. Jason answers.
3. **Walking Home** — Day and Jason talk about what they just saw.

**Key Expression Area:** code-switch / blend / naturally / pick up on / without thinking / grow into

---

### EP64 "Date Night"
**Story:** Jason과 Day의 첫 제대로 된 데이트 나이트. 케지니가 사나를 봐줌. 레스토랑에서 — 사나, 일, 걱정 없이 그냥 두 사람 얘기.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **Getting Ready** — Kenji agrees to watch Sana. They get dressed up properly.
2. **The Restaurant** — First 10 minutes they keep talking about Sana. Then Day says: "What do you actually want to talk about?"
3. **Walking Back** — They don't rush home.

**Key Expression Area:** be present / catch up / without the usual context / side of someone / reconnect / slow down

---

### EP65 "Marcus's New Chapter"
**Story:** Marcus가 Jason에게 사귀는 사람이 생겼다고 알림. 처음으로 일 얘기가 아닌 개인 얘기를 나눔. 이 우정이 어떻게 깊어졌는지 Jason이 반추.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Mention** — Marcus casually mentions someone. Jason catches it.
2. **The Real Conversation** — Marcus opens up more than usual. Jason listens.
3. **After** — Jason reflects: a year ago Marcus was barely a coworker. Now this.

**Key Expression Area:** open up / trust someone with / earn (trust) / beyond the surface / unexpected friendship / mean something

---

### EP66 "Kenji's Story"
**Story:** Kenji가 처음으로 자신의 이민 이야기를 Jason에게 들려줌. 부모님이 70년대 일본에서 왔음. Jason은 Kenji가 왜 항상 이민자들에게 친절한지 이해하게 됨.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Setup** — Kenji and Jason in the hallway after he watches Sana.
2. **Kenji's Parents** — Kenji shares his family's story for the first time.
3. **Jason Hears It** — Jason processes: "Everyone in this building came from somewhere."

**Key Expression Area:** root (noun/verb) / come from somewhere / carry (history) / pass down / belong / recognize yourself in

---

### EP67 "The Bigger Mistake"
**Story:** 더 큰 업무 실수 — EP46보다 규모가 크지만 Jason의 반응이 다름. 즉시 Patel에게 보고, 해결책 함께 제시. 그날 저녁 Day와: "A year ago this would have broken me for a week."

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Error** — Jason catches a significant data discrepancy in a shipment report he filed.
2. **The Response** — He goes to Patel immediately, with a solution already drafted.
3. **Evening** — Day: "How are you?" Jason explains the difference from last time.

**Key Expression Area:** get ahead of (a problem) / own it / come prepared / break down / bounce back / not the same person

---

### EP68 "Day's Project Results"
**Story:** Day의 서울 프로젝트 성공적 마무리. 클라이언트가 연장 제안. Day가 Jason에게: "I want to keep going." Jason의 반응 — 이번엔 로지스틱스 걱정 없이 바로 지지.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Call** — Day gets off a Seoul call looking satisfied.
2. **The News** — She tells Jason: client extended for 3 more months, and there may be more after.
3. **Jason's Response** — Immediate support. "Of course. Let's figure out the mornings."

**Key Expression Area:** see it through / extend / keep going / invest in / long game / make room for

---

### EP69 "A Letter Home"
**Story:** Jason이 부모님께 편지를 씀 (실제로 보내는지는 모호). 지금 자신의 상태를 한국에 있는 부모님이 이해할 수 있는 언어로 설명하려 함. Day가 편지 쓰는 걸 보고: "You should actually send it."

**3 Scenes:**
1. **Writing** — Jason alone, writing. We hear his internal voice as he tries to explain Houston in Korean terms his parents would understand.
2. **Day Reads It** — She asks to read it. She does. Quietly: "Send it."
3. **Hitting Send** — He emails it. Waits. "They'll reply when they're ready."

**Key Expression Area:** put into words / across the distance / bridge the gap / translated (feeling, not language) / reach / find the right words
