## 에피소드 스토리 개요

### EP57 "The Second Summer"

**Story:** Houston의 두 번째 여름. Jason이 David에게 문자 — "Last year I was surviving the heat. This year it's just Tuesday." Marcus와 점심, 오피스에서 1년 전과 달라진 자신을 실감. Day와 저녁에 이 변화에 대해.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Text** — Jason texts David from the office break room. Reflects on how Houston summer felt like a threat last year vs. just weather now.
2. **Lunch with Marcus** — Marcus notices Jason seems different. Jason articulates what changed.
3. **Evening: Day** — Jason and Day on the balcony. "Year two feels like we finally own this."

**Jason 핵심 라인 예시:**
> "Last summer I was fighting Houston. This summer Houston is just the background."

**Expression Bank 주제어 (6–8개):**
- settle in / feel at home / get used to / in the background / take for granted / second nature / own (a place/feeling) / the long way around

---

### EP58 "Feedback Season"

**Story:** FreshLine 첫 공식 성과 리뷰. Patel이 Jason의 이중 체크 프로토콜이 팀 표준이 됐다고 알려줌. Jason이 David에게 전화로 알림. Day와 "이제 뭘 목표로 해야 하지?" 대화.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **Patel's Office** — Annual review. Patel says: "The double-check protocol you built after the inventory error — we adopted it team-wide. That's yours."
2. **The Call** — Jason calls David (Austin) to tell him. David: "I told you."
3. **Home** — Jason and Day: what does the next milestone look like?

**Jason 핵심 라인 예시:**
> "A mistake I made became the thing that made the team more careful, which means the mistake was worth something — not because I planned it that way, but because I didn't bury it."

**Expression Bank 주제어:**
- give credit / set a standard / own a mistake / build on / be recognized / raise the bar / put something behind you / pay off

---

### EP59 "Video Call Home"

**Story:** 오랜만에 부모님과 영상통화. Jason의 엄마가 사나에게 한국어로 말 걸기 시도. 사나가 "할머니"라고 대답. Jason이 전화 끊고 나서 감정 정리 — "그 두 사람은 만난 적이 없는데 언어로 이어졌어."

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Call** — Parents on screen. Dad asks how work is. Mom talks to Sana.
2. **Sana Says It** — Sana says "할머니" clearly. Everyone's reaction.
3. **After the Call** — Jason and Day. Jason processes the moment.

**Jason 핵심 라인 예시:**
> "My mother spoke to Sana in Korean and Sana answered, which means the language did something I couldn't engineer — it connected two people who have never been in the same room."

**Expression Bank 주제어:**
- reach across / bridge (a gap) / pass something down / pick up (a language) / in the same room / carry forward / without knowing it / connect
