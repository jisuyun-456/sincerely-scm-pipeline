## 에피소드 스토리 개요

### EP91 "Mom and Dad Arrive"
**Story:** 공항. Jason이 부모님을 픽업. 처음으로 부모님이 Houston에 있음. 차 안에서 — 2년 만의 대화.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **Arrivals** — Jason waiting at the airport. The moment he sees them.
2. **The Car** — First conversation. Dad looking out the window. Mom asking about Sana.
3. **The Apartment Door** — Day and Sana waiting. Sana looks at her grandparents.

**Key Line:** "They walked out of arrivals and I felt two worlds touch each other for the first time."

---

### EP92 "The City Tour"
**Story:** Jason이 부모님에게 Houston을 보여줌. H-Mart, FreshLine 건물 밖, 그들의 아파트 동네. "이게 내가 2년 동안 살던 곳이야."

**3 Scenes:**
1. **H-Mart** — Mom immediately at home. Dad quietly observing.
2. **The Office Building** — Jason points it out. Dad nods.
3. **The Neighborhood Walk** — Jason narrates the last 2 years in physical locations.

**Key Line:** "I was showing them a map of the last two years. Every place had a story."

---

### EP93 "Mom and Sana"
**Story:** 엄마와 사나의 만남. 처음엔 서먹함. 그러다 사나가 "할머니"라고 부름. 엄마가 우는 순간.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **Morning** — Mom and Sana at breakfast. Sana studies her grandmother.
2. **The Word** — Sana reaches for Jason's mom and says "할머니."
3. **After** — Mom in the kitchen. Jason finds her. "She knew the word."

**Callback:** EP59 (영상통화에서 사나가 처음 "할머니" 말했던 순간) — 이제 실제 만남

---

### EP94 "Dad's Observation"
**Story:** Jason 아버지가 사위처럼 조용히 관찰하다가 한 번, 조용하게 Jason에게 말함: "잘 됐다." 이 한마디가 Jason에게 큰 의미.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Walk** — Jason and Dad walk alone while Day and Mom are with Sana.
2. **The Quiet** — They don't say much. Then Dad says: "잘 됐다."
3. **Jason Processes** — He tells Day that night. "That's the most my dad has ever said about how I'm doing."

**Key Line:** "My father doesn't give speeches. But he says what he means, and what he said was: I see what you built, and it's good."

---

### EP95 "The Last Night Together"
**Story:** 부모님 방문 마지막 날 밤. 온 가족이 식탁에. 특별한 대화 없이도 모든 게 완성되는 느낌.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **Dinner** — All five of them (Jason, Day, Sana, Mom, Dad). Mostly in Korean.
2. **After Dinner** — The adults talk. Sana falls asleep in Grandma's arms.
3. **Before Bed** — Jason alone, briefly. "I've been waiting for this moment without knowing it."

---

### EP96 "After They Leave"
**Story:** 공항 배웅 후. Jason이 돌아오는 차 안에서, 집에서. 이번 작별은 다름 — 슬프지만 무너지지 않음.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Airport Goodbye** — Hugs. Mom holds Sana one more time.
2. **The Drive Home** — Quiet. Day doesn't say anything. Neither does Jason.
3. **Back at the Apartment** — Sana pointing at where Grandma sat. Jason: "She'll come back."

**Key Line:** "Saying goodbye doesn't get easier. But what changes is that you're no longer afraid the goodbye means something is over."

---

### EP97 "What We Built"
**Story:** 부모님이 떠난 다음 주. Jason이 차분하게 지난 2년을 돌아봄. Day와 함께 — 어디서 시작했고 지금 어디 있는지.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Morning** — Quiet apartment. Back to normal routine.
2. **The Conversation** — Jason and Day: "What do you think changed the most?"
3. **The Answer** — Not a list, but a feeling. "We didn't survive Houston. We made a home here."

**Key Line:** "The goal was never to become American. The goal was to be us, but here. And that's what happened."

---

### EP98 "Day's Next Chapter"
**Story:** Day가 Seoul 프로젝트 이후 다음 방향을 생각하기 시작. 더 큰 기회 vs 잠깐의 휴식. Day가 Jason에게 진짜 속마음을 털어놓음.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Question** — Day: "I've been thinking about what I actually want next."
2. **The Honesty** — Day shares something she hasn't said out loud. Jason listens fully.
3. **The Permission** — "You don't need my permission. But you have it."

---

### EP99 "A Letter to Sana"
**Story:** Jason이 미래의 사나에게 편지를 씀. 10년 후, 20년 후의 사나에게. 이 시리즈 전체를 편지 형식으로 압축.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Start** — Jason at his desk, late. He starts writing.
2. **The Middle** — We hear the letter: what he wants her to know about these years.
3. **Sana Asleep** — He looks in on her. "I'm writing to someone I haven't met yet."

**Key Line:** "By the time you read this, you won't remember Houston the way I remember it. But it will be in you — in your Korean, in the way you move between languages, in the fact that you know what it means to belong to more than one place."

> **Format note:** Jason's letter can be slightly different structure — no dialogue, just his voice in letter format. Still 3 scenes, still per-sentence line breaks.

---
