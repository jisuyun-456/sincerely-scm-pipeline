## 에피소드 스토리 개요

### EP71 "Jason Applies for Promotion"
**Story:** Jason이 승진을 직접 요청하기로 결심. EP43 (Asking for a Raise)의 성장 버전. 그때는 준비했지만 두려웠다면, 이번엔 자격이 있다는 걸 안다.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Decision** — Jason tells Day he's going to ask. She says: "Good."
2. **Patel's Office** — Jason makes his case. Not defensive, not over-prepared. Just clear.
3. **The Wait** — Back at his desk. David on the phone: "How'd it go?" "I said what I meant."

**Key Callback:** EP43 "Asking for a Raise" — 그때와 지금의 차이를 Jason이 명시적으로 비교

---

### EP72 "Day Extends the Contract"
**Story:** Day의 Seoul 프로젝트가 다시 3개월 연장. 이번엔 더 큰 범위. Day가 Jason에게 알리는 방식이 달라짐 — 이젠 허락을 구하는 게 아니라 함께 계획하는 것.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The News** — Day tells Jason casually. He's already thinking logistics.
2. **The Plan** — They map out the next 3 months together: Sana mornings, timing.
3. **Quiet Moment** — Day: "You've gotten good at this." Jason: "At what?" Day: "Sharing."

---

### EP73 "The Third Summer"
**Story:** 세 번째 여름. 더 이상 적응의 계절이 아니라 그냥 삶의 일부. 공원, 아이스크림, Sana. Houston이 집처럼 느껴지는 물리적 순간들.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Morning** — Family routine: Sana, breakfast, Jason's commute. Nothing dramatic, everything comfortable.
2. **The Park** — Spontaneous detour after work. The three of them.
3. **Walking Home** — Jason: "I stop noticing the heat now. It's just weather."

**Key Line:** "The city stopped being something I was adjusting to and became something I just live in."

---

### EP74 "Language Milestone"
**Story:** Sana가 처음으로 완전한 한국어 문장을 말함. 짧지만 완전한 문장. Jason이 완전히 얼어붙는 순간.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Sentence** — Sana says a full Korean sentence (e.g., "아빠, 나 배고파"). Context: Jason asking what she wants for dinner.
2. **Jason and Day React** — Both freeze, then quietly celebrate (not overdoing it in front of Sana).
3. **That Night** — Jason and Day: what this means for the language plan going forward.

**Key Line:** "She said it like it was nothing, which means it was already natural to her. That's the whole goal."

---

### EP75 "David Visits"
**Story:** David가 Austin에서 주말에 놀러 옴. 처음으로 Jason의 실제 삶 — 아파트, 사나, Day — 을 보는 David. "This is different from what I pictured."

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Arrival** — David at the apartment door. Sana immediately interested in him.
2. **The Saturday** — The four of them (+ maybe Kenji) spend the day. David sees Jason in his element.
3. **Sunday Morning** — David and Jason alone. "You built something real here."

**Callback:** EP56 "David's News" — 헤어질 때와 지금 다시 만남

---

### EP76 "The Parents Question"
**Story:** Jason 부모님이 방문 계획을 언급. Jason이 Houston에서 부모님을 맞이하는 걸 상상. 설렘과 불안이 공존 — "What will they think of who I am here?"

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Call** — Jason's mom mentions coming to visit. No date yet. Jason says "Yes, come."
2. **Jason and Day** — What does it feel like to think about showing them this life?
3. **Before Sleep** — Jason imagines the visit: what he wants them to see, what he's proud of.

---

### EP77 "The Career Conversation"
**Story:** Jason과 Day — 다음 5년 계획 대화. 자연스럽게 나온 대화. 정착 vs 성장, 미국에 계속 있을 것인지. 결론은 열린 채로 끝나지만 불안에서 비롯된 게 아님.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Question** — Day: "Do you see yourself here in five years?" Not accusatory — genuine.
2. **The Real Answer** — Jason works through it out loud.
3. **The Agreement** — No decision, but they're asking from a position of strength, not fear.

**Key Line:** "We're not talking about this because we're unhappy. We're talking about it because we get to choose."

---

### EP78 "Day's Korean Network"
**Story:** Day가 Seoul 프로젝트를 통해 한국 내 전문가 네트워크를 만들고 있음. 이 부분의 Day가 Jason에게 처음으로 자신의 직업적 삶을 상세히 공유.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Briefing** — Day explains her Seoul project context to Jason in detail — first time she's opened this much.
2. **Jason Listens** — He asks real questions. Day is surprised by his understanding.
3. **The Realization** — Jason: "I've been supporting this project without really knowing what it was. Now I do."

---

### EP79 "The American Dream"
**Story:** 한국 친척이 "미국에 살면 아메리칸 드림 이루었겠다"고 함. Jason이 이 말을 가지고 씨름. 그 단어가 자신에게 어떤 의미인지, 아닌지.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Comment** — A relative on a group chat says it. Jason pauses.
2. **The Thinking** — Jason and Day discuss: what does that phrase even mean for them?
3. **The Resolution** — Not the phrase they'd use, but something closer to "we built something on purpose."

**Key Line:** "I don't know if this is the American Dream. But it's the life we made deliberately, which feels like more."
