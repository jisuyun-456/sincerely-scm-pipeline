## 에피소드 스토리 개요

### EP81 "The Promotion"
**Story:** 승진 통보. EP71 (신청)의 결과. Jason이 Patel에게 불려감. 예상했지만 실제로 듣는 순간은 다름. David에게 전화, Day에게.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **Patel's Office** — The announcement. Patel: "You asked for this, and you earned it."
2. **The Call** — Jason calls David from the parking garage.
3. **Home** — Day already somehow knew. "I've been watching you become this person all year."

**Callback:** EP43 (첫 인상 요청), EP71 (승진 신청) — 이 3개가 하나의 아크

---

### EP82 "Day's Big Project"
**Story:** Day의 Seoul 클라이언트가 더 큰 한국 시장 론칭 프로젝트를 맡김. Day가 이걸 받을지 망설이는 모습 — 처음으로. Jason이 역할을 뒤집어 Day를 설득.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Offer** — Day tells Jason. She's actually uncertain — bigger scope, more time.
2. **The Reversal** — Jason gives the support speech. "You know how to do this."
3. **Day Decides** — She says yes. Then: "You've gotten good at this too."

**Key Line:** "A year ago you were giving me the space I needed. I can do that now too."

---

### EP83 "Marcus Gets Engaged"
**Story:** Marcus가 약혼 소식을 Jason에게 먼저 알림 (직장에서). 이 순간이 두 사람의 우정이 얼마나 진짜가 됐는지를 보여줌.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The News** — Marcus at Jason's desk: "I asked her." Jason's response.
2. **The Story** — Marcus tells Jason how it happened. Jason listens like a real friend.
3. **Reflection** — Jason to Day that night: "He told me first."

**Callback:** EP42 (The Difficult Conversation) — 그 대화가 이 우정의 시작이었음

---

### EP84 "Cooking Together"
**Story:** Jason이 사나에게 한국 음식 만드는 방법을 가르치려 함 — 당연히 사나는 2살이라 진짜 요리는 못 함. 그래도 옆에 두고 냄새 맡게 하고, 재료 설명하고. 부모님 방문 준비의 일환.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Setup** — Jason in the kitchen. Sana in her chair nearby.
2. **The Lesson** — Jason narrates what he's making in Korean. Sana pokes things.
3. **Day Watches** — Day from the doorway. "Your mother taught you this." "Yeah. Now I'm passing it on."

**Key Line:** "You can't teach a two-year-old to cook. But you can let them be in the kitchen with you, and that's where it starts."

---

### EP85 "The Korean Community"
**Story:** Houston에 한인 커뮤니티가 있다는 걸 이제야 제대로 발견. 어떤 행사 / 식당 / 모임에 처음 참석. "이게 여기 있었는데 1년 동안 몰랐어."

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Discovery** — Day finds a Korean community event. They go together.
2. **At the Event** — Surrounded by Korean, Korean food, Korean-Americans. Sana in Jason's arms.
3. **Walking Out** — Jason: "I didn't know I needed this until I was in it."

---

### EP86 "Teaching Sana Korean"
**Story:** Jason이 의도적으로 사나에게 한국어를 가르치는 저녁 루틴을 만들기 시작. 그림책, 단어, 노래. 부모님이 왔을 때 사나가 한국어로 대화할 수 있게.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Books** — Korean picture books from H-Mart. Bedtime routine changes.
2. **The Song** — Jason sings a Korean children's song (어릴 때 엄마가 불러줬던). Sana listens.
3. **Day Joins** — They do it together. "This is what language transmission actually is."

**Key Line:** "I'm not teaching her a language. I'm teaching her a world."

---

### EP87 "Kenji Moves Away"
**Story:** Kenji가 딸 근처 (다른 도시)로 이사 결정. Jason에게 직접 알림. 첫 Houston 이웃의 마지막.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The News** — Kenji tells Jason in the hallway. His daughter's family nearby.
2. **The Last Favor** — Kenji asks if they can watch his plant while he packs. Final conversation.
3. **After He Leaves** — Jason to Day: "That's the second time." Day: "It's different this time." Jason: "Yeah. I'm the one staying."

**Callback:** EP56 (David leaves), 이번엔 Jason이 남는 쪽

---

### EP88 "The FreshLine Future"
**Story:** FreshLine Foods가 조직 개편. Jason의 역할이 변할 수 있음. 불확실성 속에서 Jason의 반응이 2년 전과 완전히 다름.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The Announcement** — Company restructure. Jason's team may change.
2. **The Old Feeling vs New** — Jason notices he's not panicking. Curious instead.
3. **With Day** — "A year ago I would have spiraled on this. Now it just feels like information."

---

### EP89 "Before the Visit"
**Story:** 부모님 방문 1주일 전. Jason이 준비하는 것들 — 음식, 아파트, 무엇보다 "내가 여기서 어떤 사람인지 보여주는 것". 감정적 준비.

**3 Scenes:**
1. **The List** — Jason and Day making practical preparations.
2. **The Real Preparation** — Jason: "I want them to see what we've built."
3. **The Night Before** — Jason can't sleep. Day: "They're coming to see you. Not the apartment."

**Key Line:** "I've been building a life here for two years. Next week someone I love is finally going to see it."
